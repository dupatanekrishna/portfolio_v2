What was implemented

Validation approach

dig CNAME krishna.dupatane.portfolio.dkalki.com +short
curl -IL https://krishna.dupatane.portfolio.dkalki.com/
curl -Iv https://krishna.dupatane.portfolio.dkalki.com/

Validation focused on routing, redirects, certificate trust and the distinction between domain ownership, DNS visibility and HTTPS readiness.

Private verification TXT values, registrar data, credentials and account-specific screenshots are intentionally excluded from the public portfolio.

Engineering outcomes

  • Explain registrar, DNS provider and authoritative nameserver roles.
  • Configure and validate GitHub Pages custom-domain routing.
  • Troubleshoot DNS and resolver cache behavior.
  • Validate HTTPS redirects and TLS certificate behavior.
  • Differentiate ownership verification from DNS and HTTPS validation.
  • Reason about MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC for custom-domain email.
